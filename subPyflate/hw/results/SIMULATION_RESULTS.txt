Compilation notes:
  Icarus prints one warning at rtl/huffman_decoder.sv line 128
  ("sorry: constant selects in always_* processes are not currently
  supported (all bits will be included)"). This refers to the truncation
  `lut_rd_bits[SHIFT_CONS_W-1:0]` in the S_CONSUME state.
  Icarus falls back to passing the full 5-bit value, which is then
  truncated implicitly at the 4-bit assignment to `bs_consume`. The
  observed behaviour is identical, and the decoder produces the correct
  decoded symbol stream (tb_huffman_decoder DECODER RESULT: ALL PASS).
