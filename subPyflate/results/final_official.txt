Speedup: 1.70x (using time-elapsed means).
Improvement: **41.10%** (well above the >=7% target).

Interpretation:
- Wall-clock time drops by 41% between original and final. task-clock
  drops less (33%) because pyperformance's warmup/manager overhead is
  identical in both runs. Both metrics show the same trend.
- page-fault count is nearly identical between original and final,
  showing the optimization removed *interpreter dispatches*, not
  memory allocations. Consistent with the Attempt-3 hoisting cleanup.
- The 12.6% stddev on ORIGINAL vs 1.4% on FINAL reflects TCG's
  higher scheduling jitter when the workload is longer; the final
  optimized workload is short enough to be stable.
- Hardware PMU counters unavailable under TCG (documented in
  docs/03_profiling.md).
