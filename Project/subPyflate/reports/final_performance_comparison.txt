--- Windows preliminary (2026-09-20, time.perf_counter, custom loop) ---
Source: results/windows_preliminary.txt (scripts/windows_bench.py, 8 iterations x 3 loops)

  original: 1.8877 s  (baseline)
  attempt1: 2.4883 s  (-31.82%)  <-- cold-loop LUT-build cost dominates
  attempt2: 1.7626 s  (+6.63%)
  attempt3: 1.6855 s  (+10.71%)
  final:    1.6394 s  (+13.16%)

Interpretation of the two Windows measurements:
- pyperformance uses pyperf's warmed-up, calibrated methodology and
  amortizes LUT-build cost across many iterations, so Attempt 1 shows a
  clean +16% win.
- The simple time.perf_counter script measures cold-start decompressions,
  where the LUT-build in Python (up to 131K entries per Huffman group)
  dominates and Attempt 1 shows a regression.
- Both agree Attempt 3 / final is clearly fastest and above the 7% target.
- This split validates the hardware-acceleration argument: the LUT concept
  works algorithmically, but Python-side build overhead is heavy in some
  scenarios; hardware BRAM has O(1) load AND O(1) lookup.
